**Quarantine** is a holding area for messages Nullbox decides are unsafe or untrusted to deliver directly.

The goal is simple:

- keep questionable mail out of your inbox, and
- let you tighten or relax protection without losing visibility into what happened

## Common reasons messages are quarantined

- **Unknown sender** (especially when Active protection is enabled)
- **Suspicious sender** (a conservative safety heuristic)
- **Policy** (a rule or control decided the message should not be delivered directly)

## What to do when something is quarantined

If you expected the message:

1. Check the mailbox/alias activity feed to confirm it was quarantined and see the reason.
2. If you need it delivered immediately, temporarily enable **Learning mode** or **Direct passthrough** and ask the sender to resend.
3. After you’ve received what you need, switch back to **Active protection** if the alias is sensitive.

If you did not expect the message:

- Leave protection enabled, and consider disabling the alias if it is being abused.

## Privacy note

Quarantine is the one place where retaining message content may be necessary so you can review it. For forwarded/dropped messages, Nullbox focuses on delivery metadata and audit records rather than storing full message bodies.

See [Data and privacy](/reference/data-and-privacy) for details.
